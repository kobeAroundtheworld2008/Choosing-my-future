Introduction

One thing I often think about is my future and the career I want to pursue. I am currently taking Information Technology, but sometimes I wonder if this is really the path I want. Deep inside, I have always imagined myself becoming an airline pilot. This blog is about my dreams, my decisions, and the uncertainty of choosing my future.

The Path I Chose

I chose IT because I saw it as one of the better options for my future, even though it wasn't my dream course. I don't regret my decision, but sometimes I question whether I am following the path I truly want.

The Dream I Have

If I had the chance to choose between working in IT and becoming an airline pilot, I would choose aviation without hesitation. The idea of flying, exploring different places, and seeing the world from above has always fascinated me. I know it won't be easy, but it is a dream I still hope to achieve someday.

My Future Is Still Unwritten

I don't know exactly where life will take me. Maybe I will build my career in IT, or maybe I will find a way to pursue aviation. What matters is that I don't completely forget what I truly want.

To other students who are also unsure about their future, remember that it's okay not to have everything figured out. We still have time to discover ourselves, make decisions, and follow the dreams that inspire us.
